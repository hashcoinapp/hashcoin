Your antivirus stopped mining

Mining software often gets flagged because it uses your graphics card the way some malware does. Antivirus programs guess here; they don't know. Hashcoin isn't code-signed, so they have less to go on.

The installer's SHA-256 is published on the website, and the code is public on GitHub. Miner files only run if they match their published SHA-256. Hashcoin never changes your antivirus or security settings.

Allowing it is your choice. To allow it:

  1. Open Windows Security.
  2. Go to Virus & threat protection → Protection history.
  3. Select the Hashcoin item, then Actions → Allow on device. If there are two (the app and the miner), allow both.
  4. Reopen Hashcoin.
  1. Open your antivirus.
  2. Find Hashcoin in its quarantine or history.
  3. Restore it, then allow it or add it as an exception. If there are two (the app and the miner), do both.
  4. Reopen Hashcoin.

Welcome to Hashcoin

Your graphics card mines when you let it. What it mines buys $HASH, and you're paid in $HASH.

Setup takes a minute. Nothing starts until you press Start.

What's your Solana address?

Your payouts go here, in $HASH. Copy it from your wallet app, such as Phantom, Solflare or Backpack.

Your public address only. Never your seed phrase or private key.

Hashcoin never asks for them. Anyone who does is trying to take your wallet.

How you get paid

  • Mining. Your card mines whatever pays most. That coin is sold to buy $HASH for you.
  • Payouts. $HASH goes straight to your wallet. A 2–5% tax on every trade pays miners and holders.
  • Levels. Hold $50 of $HASH for Level 1: holder rewards and the miner bonus. $500 is Level 2 and $2,500 is Level 3.
  • Selling counts 3×. Sell $100 and your bag counts $300 less for your level, until you buy back.

Earnings are estimates. They move with coin prices and trading.

Your antivirus may step in

Antivirus programs often flag mining software, because it uses the graphics card the way some malware does. They guess; they don't know.

Hashcoin isn't code-signed, so they have less to go on. If one stops it, the app tells you what happened and how to allow it, if you choose to.

Hashcoin never changes your antivirus or security settings.

Miner files only run if they match their published SHA-256, and the code is public on GitHub.
