    MiningBonus

    Values are in dollars of $HASH at payout prices, before pool fees and slippage. About 75% of mining is paid every ~10 minutes; the rest after the mined coin confirms and sells, trued up to the real price. The tax (2–5%, set by a public formula) splits into 5–10% development, a miner chest that targets 5× miners' mining (45–75%), and holder rewards (the rest, at least 1% of every trade). Your level comes from your bag and counts for both holder rewards and the miner bonus; mining without a bag gets no bonus. The estimate assumes you don't sell: selling counts 3× against your bag, so it lowers your level. Only the rules are fixed (1 : 2 : 4 weights, square-root balancing for miners); the dollar amounts are not.

    The math behind the estimate

    Earnings depend on one ratio: daily volume ÷ GPUs mining. The estimate uses $750 per GPU.

    1. The tax rate r is the lowest of 2–5% that lifts miners to 5× and leaves holders 1% or more of volume.

      tax = r × (V + N × a)

    2. Development gets 5–10% of it (less when the tax is higher). Miners with a bag (share b) get what lifts their average to 5×, kept within 45–75%.

      chest ÷ N = 4 × a × b, within 45–75% of tax ÷ N

    3. Your bonus: √(your GPU's $ a day) × your level, against the average miner with a bag. No bag, no bonus.

      bonus = chest ÷ N ÷ b × √(your GPU) × level ÷ (√a × L)

    4. Holders get the rest. Only V ÷ N matters.

      holders ÷ N = r × (V ÷ N + a) − dev ÷ N − chest ÷ N

    V daily volume, N GPUs mining, a the average GPU's $ a day, b the share of miners with a bag, L their average level.

    Why $750 per GPU

    V ÷ N = 30% × $1,000 ÷ 40% = $750

    Volume ÷ market cap
    30% a day, a new Solana token's first weeks. Usually lower later.
    Market cap ÷ holder
    $1,000. A $1M token has about 1,000 holders.
    Holders mining
    40% of holders run the app.
    Average GPU
    a = $3.50 a day, about an RTX 3080.
    Levels
    b = 80% of miners hold a bag: 25% Level 1, 35% Level 2, 20% Level 3, so L = 2.2.

    The 5× target is for the average miner with a bag. Square-root balancing gives smaller cards a bigger multiple of what they mine and the fastest cards a smaller one: at $750 per GPU, an RTX 3080 at Level 3 makes about 8× its mining and an RTX 5090 about 4.5×.
