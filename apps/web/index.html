    How it works

    Mining creates steady buying. Trading pays the people who mine and hold.

    1. GPUs mine and buy $HASH

      The app mines whichever coin pays your GPU most. What it mines is sold and turned into market buys of $HASH.

      RTX 4070, mining Pearl$4.45 a day
      Sold, then a market buy$4.45 of $HASH

      Revenue per card: hashrate.no, Oct 14, 2026.

    2. Every trade pays a 2–5% tax

      Buys, sells and transfers all pay it, on every exchange, because the token itself charges it. A public formula sets the rate.

      Buy $820$24.60 tax
      Sell $240$7.20 tax
      Buy $1,950$58.50 tax

    3. Miners and holders get paid

      The tax goes out in $HASH: a bonus for miners who hold a bag, and rewards for everyone holding $50 or more.

        FAQ

        Do I need a GPU?

        No. Hold $50 or more and you share the holder rewards from every trade. Miners' share is capped, so busier trading means more for holders.

        Do I need to buy $HASH to mine?

        No. Mining pays you in $HASH. The miner bonus starts once your bag reaches $50, and the $HASH you mine counts toward it.

        What happens if I sell?

        You pay the tax (2–5%), and the sale counts 3× against your bag: sell $100 of a $500 bag and it counts as $200 until you buy back. That can lower your level and your rewards.

        How much will I make?

        It depends on trading volume and how many GPUs mine. The calculator uses launch-week trading. After launch it uses what was actually paid in the last 24 hours.The calculator above uses what was actually paid in the last 24 hours. Nothing here is a promise.

        Will mining slow down my PC?

        No. The miner only uses GPU power you aren't using. When a game or render needs the GPU, mining steps back and only your hashrate drops.

        Is this a rug?

        The tax follows a public formula that keeps it between 2% and 5%, and any change shows on-chain about 4 days before it applies. The dev bag is 1%, bought in public into one public wallet, and gets no payouts. Every payout is on-chain.
